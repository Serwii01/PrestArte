About the Work

This seminal work represents a pivotal moment in the artist's "Blue Period," exploring themes of melancholy and structural abstraction. Acquired directly from the artist's estate in 1960, this piece has been exhibited in major retrospectives across Europe and North America. The canvas retains its original vibrancy, with minimal restoration intervention documented. Ideal for thematic exhibitions focusing on mid-century color theory.
